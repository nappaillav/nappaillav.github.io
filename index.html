I'm a researcher broadly interested in robot learning, hierarchical world models, and reinforcement learning. My work focuses on building agents that can plan and act over long horizons in complex environments. I am part of the Mobile Robotics Lab at McGill, advised by David Meger.
Previously, I explored model-based RL and offline learning methods. I enjoy thinking about how structure in the world can be used to make learning more efficient and generalizable.
Feel free to reach out at valliappan0808@gmail.com.

    Hierarchical Planning for Excavators
    Built an off-the-shelf world model for hierarchical planning over long-horizon tasks on an excavator simulator. The system accepts natural-language instructions and decomposes them into primitive actions, enabling flexible, language-grounded task execution.
